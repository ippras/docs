# Sum triacylglycerols by chain length

- Short chain triacylglycerols (SCTs) are triacylglycerols with aliphatic tails of 5 or fewer carbons (e.g. butyric acid).[7]
- Medium chain triacylglycerols (MCTs) are triacylglycerols with aliphatic tails of 6 to 12 carbons, which can form medium-chain triglycerides.[8]
- Long chain triacylglycerols (LCTs) are triacylglycerols with aliphatic tails of 13 to 21 carbons.[9]
- Very long chain triacylglycerols (VLCTs) are triacylglycerols with aliphatic tails of 22 or more carbons.
