# Fish lipid quality

<!-- i18n:skip -->
$$\frac{20:5[\omega-3] + 22:6[\omega-3]}{\sum FA}$$

## Abbreviations

- FLQ

## Synonyms

- Flesh lipid quality

## Description

Calculates the sum of EPA and DHA as a percentage of total fatty acids.
